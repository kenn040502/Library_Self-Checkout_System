### Lessons Evidence
LESSONS LEARNED User-Friendly UI: Designing a user interface that works consistently across both mobile and desktop environments proved to be one of the most challenging aspects of Sprint 3. Selecting a cohesive colour theme that functions equally well in light mode and dark mode required careful consideration of contrast ratios, readability, and visual hierarchy at every breakpoint. The team learned that small decisions such as which shade of cream to use for backgrounds, or how aggressively to round button corners have an outsized effect on whether the interface feels polished or amateurish. Button placement and the logical grouping of interactive elements also required multiple iterations: what felt intuitive to the developer building a feature was not always obvious to a first-time user. The team now understands that UX decisions should be evaluated from the perspective of a student encountering a page for the first time, not from the perspective of someone who built it.
